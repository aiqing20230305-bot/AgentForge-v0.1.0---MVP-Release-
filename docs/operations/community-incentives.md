# 🎖️ AgentForge Community Incentives Program

## 🌟 Overview

The AgentForge Community Incentives Program rewards contributors who help grow our ecosystem through code, content, support, and advocacy. Whether you're a developer, writer, designer, or community champion, there's a place for you!

**Mission:** Build the most engaged, helpful, and innovative AI agent community in the world.

## 📈 How Points are Calculated

### Contribution Points Formula

**Code Contributions:**
- Small PR merged: 10 points
- Medium PR merged: 25 points
- Large PR merged: 50 points
- Critical bug fix: 100 points
- Code review: 5 points

**Community Engagement:**
- Helpful message: 2 points
- Question answered: 5 points
- Thread resolved: 10 points
- Mentoring session: 20 points

**Content Creation:**
- Tutorial: 50 points
- Blog post: 40 points
- Video tutorial: 60 points
- Documentation: 20 points
- Translation: 30 points

**Plugin Development:**
- Plugin published: 100 points
- Plugin update: 20 points
- Per 100 downloads: 50 points
- 5-star review: 10 points

**Event Organization:**
- Meetup hosted: 75 points
- Workshop led: 100 points
- Hackathon organized: 200 points

### Multipliers

**Quality Bonus:** x1.5 for exceptional work
**Consistency Bonus:** x1.2 for 3+ months active
**First-Time Bonus:** x2 for first contribution in category
**Community Choice:** x1.3 for community-nominated work

### Point Tiers

- **Explorer:** 0-99 points
- **Builder:** 100-499 points
- **Expert:** 500-1,999 points
- **Legend:** 2,000+ points
