# 💰 AgentForge Monetization Strategy

## 🎯 Executive Summary

AgentForge will operate on a freemium model with three tiers: Free (open source core), Pro (power users), and Enterprise (teams and businesses). This strategy balances community growth with sustainable revenue while maintaining our commitment to open source.

**Revenue Goal:** $1M ARR by end of Year 1

**Conversion Target:** 2% free → paid within 6 months

---
